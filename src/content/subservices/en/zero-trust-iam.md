Passwords shared in chat, accounts that outlive the employee, admin rights handed out "for now". Identity is where most breaches begin, and where the fix is most visible.

We connect your directory, roll out SSO and MFA in stages, define roles so access follows the job, and monitor sign-ins so unusual behaviour is caught early.
