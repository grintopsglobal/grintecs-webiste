faq:
  - q: What is the difference between a vulnerability assessment and a penetration
      test?
    a: A vulnerability assessment scans your systems and lists known weaknesses. A
      penetration test goes further. Our testers try to exploit those weaknesses,
      including chaining several of them, to show how far an attacker could
      actually get. VAPT combines both.
  - q: Is VAPT safe for production systems?
    a: Yes. We use controlled methods and agree the scope and timing with you to
      prevent downtime.
  - q: What do blackbox, greybox, and whitebox mean?
    a: They describe how much access we start with. Blackbox means no credentials
      or internal information, just like an outside attacker. Greybox uses a
      regular user account, such as a customer or employee login. Whitebox gives
      full access, usually including architecture documents and source code, for
      the deepest coverage.
  - q: How long does a VAPT project take?
    a: It depends on the scope. Starter and Business packages take up to one
      month, Enterprise up to two months. We agree the exact schedule with you
      before testing starts.
  - q: What do we need to prepare?
    a: A list of targets in scope (domains, IP addresses, or applications), a
      testing window, and one technical contact on your side. For greybox or
      whitebox testing, we also need test accounts or the relevant documentation.
  - q: What happens after we fix the findings?
    a: We re-test to confirm each gap is closed. Starter and Business include one
      re-test, Enterprise includes two.
  - q: How often should we run a VAPT?
    a: At least once a year, and again after major changes such as a new
      application, a large release, or an infrastructure migration. Many security
      standards and audits also require regular testing.

Automated scanners find the known issues. Manual testing finds the ones that matter: chained weaknesses, logic flaws, and misconfigurations that only show up when someone actually tries to get in.

Every finding comes with a proof of concept and a clear fix, ranked by business impact. After you patch, we test again and confirm the gap is closed.
