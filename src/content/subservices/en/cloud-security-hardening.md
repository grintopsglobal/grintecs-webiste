Most cloud incidents are not sophisticated attacks. They are open storage buckets, over-privileged accounts, and default settings nobody revisited after launch.

We inventory what you actually run, audit it against the provider's own benchmarks, tighten access, and leave monitoring in place so the posture does not drift back.
