In manufacturing, production data, raw material stock, and costs are often recorded in different systems. As a result, production planning runs on stale numbers.

We help implement manufacturing ERP, connect data from machines and the production floor, and build efficiency dashboards for supervisors and management. Security for operational technology (OT) networks is part of the design from the start.
