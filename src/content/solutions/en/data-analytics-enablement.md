Reports compiled by hand at each month end are usually late, hard to trace, and depend on one or two people.

This solution builds the flow from source systems into a data warehouse, agrees metric definitions with business owners, and presents them in dashboards that update automatically.

## Every dashboard has an owner

Each dashboard is designed for a specific decision and has a named owner. Dashboards nobody uses get reviewed or removed.
