Operations teams often drown in email, forms, and documents that must be read one by one. This solution puts AI at those points: classifying requests, extracting data from documents, and preparing replies.

Staff keep the final decision on risky cases. The system records what the AI did and who approved it.

## Common starting points

Customer service ticket classification, matching invoices to purchase orders, and finding answers in internal policy documents.
