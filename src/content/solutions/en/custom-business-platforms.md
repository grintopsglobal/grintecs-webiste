When core business processes are spread across spreadsheets, chat groups, and applications that do not talk to each other, growth means hiring people to connect them by hand.

We design and build a platform that brings those processes together, for example an ordering portal for distributors or a field app for technician teams.

## Built in stages

The first version covers the most important flow and goes into use quickly. Later features are added based on real usage.
