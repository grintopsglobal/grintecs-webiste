Every standalone system adds another export and import job. Over time, teams spend their days reconciling data between systems instead of using it.

This solution connects those systems through APIs and middleware with clear data rules: which system is the source of truth for customers, products, prices, and stock.

## Start with the costliest flow

We prioritise the data flows that are retyped most often or go wrong most often, then expand from there.
