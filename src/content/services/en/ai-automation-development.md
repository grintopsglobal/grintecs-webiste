A lot of office work is still copying data from email into a spreadsheet, then from the spreadsheet into the ERP. We start with work like that: high volume, clear rules, and results that are easy to measure.

For more complex needs, we build AI features that read documents, answer questions from an internal knowledge base, or prepare drafts for staff to check. Company data stays in an environment you control.

## How we work

Every automation has a process owner, a success metric, and a fallback path for when the system is unsure. We avoid automation that cannot be explained to an auditor or to the operations team.
