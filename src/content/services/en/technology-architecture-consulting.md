A wrong architecture decision rarely hurts in the first month. It usually shows up a year later, when the system is hard to change and infrastructure costs keep rising.

We map the systems you run today, how data flows between them, and where they are fragile. From there we design a target architecture and an order of steps that fits your budget and team capacity.

## Consulting deliverables

Architecture documents, a prioritised risk list, a comparison of options with their costs, and a quarter-by-quarter work plan. Everything is written to be read by management and used by engineers.
