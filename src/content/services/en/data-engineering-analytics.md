Meetings often go to arguing about which number is right, because each department pulls data from different systems with different formulas.

We build pipelines that collect data from those sources, clean it, and store it in one place with agreed metric definitions. Dashboards are then built on that foundation.

## A foundation for AI

Clean, documented data is also a precondition before AI models or predictive analytics can be trusted. We design for both at once.
