Off-the-shelf software works for common processes. When your process is what sets the business apart, forcing it into a standard package usually ends with a lot of manual work around the edges.

We build in short iterations. Every two weeks there is a version real users can try, so corrections happen before the cost of change grows.

## What you receive

Source code, architecture documentation, deployment scripts, and automated tests belong to you. Your internal team can continue development without depending on us.
