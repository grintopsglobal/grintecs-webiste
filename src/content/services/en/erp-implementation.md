ERP projects rarely fail because of the software. More often the master data is messy, processes have not been agreed, or users drift back to spreadsheets after go-live.

So we spend time at the start mapping processes with the people who own them, then run several migration rehearsals before a go-live date is set.

## After go-live

We support the first month-end close and track user tickets until the system is stable, then hand configuration documentation to your internal team.
