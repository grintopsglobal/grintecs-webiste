Many security incidents start with something simple: an account without MFA, a password stored in code, or a server that was never updated.

We begin with a risk assessment to decide which assets matter most, then put controls in place in that order of priority.

## Every control is tested

Every control gets tested: logins are attacked, backups are restored, and incident procedures are rehearsed with your team.
