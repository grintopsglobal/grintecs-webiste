Websites and applications need care after launch. Without regular updates, security gaps pile up and small problems only surface when customers complain.

We take over that routine work: monitoring, updating, backing up, and fixing. A monthly report explains what was done and what needs a decision.

## Systems built by others

We also take on applications built by other vendors. The first step is a code and infrastructure audit so existing risks are recorded from day one.
